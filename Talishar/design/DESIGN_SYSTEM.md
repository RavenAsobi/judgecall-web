- Accessible modal

## Responsive behavior

- Lobby: hero copy stays left aligned; setup panes stack below 900px.
- Game: log collapses first; decision panel becomes a lower sheet; hand becomes horizontally scrollable.
- Mobile: navigation is reduced, controls remain at least 44px tall, and the battlefield retains horizontal overflow rather than shrinking text below readability.
